MECE 301 · Spring 2026

Pneumatic Tube Terminal Velocity

Transient modeling and experimental validation of carrier motion in a sealed vertical pneumatic tube.

MATLABFluid MechanicsSensor CalibrationUncertainty Analysis

Objective

Predict and measure terminal velocity for carriers moving through a sealed vertical pneumatic tube.

Modeling

Developed a transient MATLAB model using viscous annular flow and isothermal ideal-gas behavior.

Measurement

Calibrated an IR sensor using a 10-point polynomial regression and checked the measurements with slow-motion video.

Result

Results met the project’s ±10% uncertainty target across 5 cm, 10 cm, and 15 cm carriers.
