Scope

Independently owned 9 mechanical test programs end-to-end across applied mechanics and reliability work.

Clamp-load measurement

Developed a capacitor-based clamp-load measurement approach for rust-preventative evaluation, calibrated it against a 10-kip vertical MTS, and used LabVIEW to acquire relevant data.

Torque-to-yield testing

Tested new and existing DIN rail end clamps to failure, processed torque-versus-angle data in Python, extracted yield torque, and used statistical comparison to evaluate performance.

Solenoid force testing

Developed a test procedure for a two-stage solenoid valve using a load cell and laser displacement measurement, with LabVIEW acquisition and force-displacement analysis.

Result

Delivered new test procedures, analysis code, and validated measurement approaches that supported design and reliability decisions.
