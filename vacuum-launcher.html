High-Velocity Vacuum Launcher

Physics-based modeling and experimental validation of a vacuum-driven launcher for a ping-pong ball projectile.

MATLABFluid MechanicsInstrumentationExperimental Validation

Objective

Design and validate a vacuum-driven launcher capable of accelerating a ping-pong ball to high velocity.

Modeling

Developed a MATLAB model incorporating compressible flow, pressure losses, adiabatic compression, viscous pipe friction, and Euler integration.

Experimental method

Measured exit velocity with an IR sensor and ballistic-block momentum method, then compared experimental results with the physics-based model and uncertainty analysis.

Result

Average measured exit velocity was approximately 196.6 m/s at 5 kPa and 172.0 m/s at 10 kPa.
